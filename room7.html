Investigation targets · Room 70/4
7.1THE TELL

Eighty signatures look like noise. They are not. Each one used a secret number called a nonce, and every nonce shares the same flaw. Work out how large the nonces can be. Format: NIGHTJAR{nonce_bits_N}, where N is the bit length that every nonce stays under.

DLDL
7.2THE KEY

The signing key can be recovered from the signatures alone. Recover the private key. Format: NIGHTJAR{d_XXXXXXXXXXXXXXXX}, the first 16 hex digits of the key, lowercase.

DLDL
7.3THE VAULT

Something was sealed with a key derived from the private key. Open it. The vault holds the answer in NIGHTJAR{...} form.

DLDL
7.4THE FORGED LINE

Log entry nightjar-log-entry-17 is the line that put a forged login under Kabir's name. Find the exact nonce its signature used. Format: NIGHTJAR{k17_XXXXXXXX}, the last 8 hex digits of that nonce, lowercase.
