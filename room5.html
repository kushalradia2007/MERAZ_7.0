5.2THE SESSION WITH TWO NAMES

JWT FORGE The portal middleware trusts user.role over data.role. The attacker had a legitimate volunteer token with two conflicting role fields. Open the JWT lab, edit user.role to admin, click FORGE TOKEN (signs it with HS256 / the correct secret), then SEND — the portal will grant the cert-renewal permission and return the flag.
