NETWORK FACTS (from the NOC runbook)

  • 10.7.7.0/24 is the operations LAN. Default gateway 10.7.7.1.
  • 10.7.7.2 is the only DNS resolver. Clients are not allowed to resolve names themselves.
  • 10.7.7.10 is the egress proxy (port 3128). All web egress must go through it; nothing else on the LAN has a route out.
  • 10.7.7.5 is the internal NTP server.
  • 10.7.7.200 is the NOC audit scanner. It ran its scheduled audit during this window.
  • Because the SPAN port sits on the switch the proxy uplinks through, you can see a connection on both sides of the proxy.

READING GUIDE

  • View > Time Display Format lets you switch between UTC and seconds since the previous displayed packet.
  • Statistics > Conversations / Endpoints / Protocol Hierarchy / Resolved Addresses give you the shape of a capture before you read a single packet.
  • Analyze > Follow rebuilds a stream. Analyze > Expert Information tells you what Wireshark thinks went wrong.
  • Display filters are case-sensitive for contains and case-insensitive for matches "(?i)...".
  • Every question in this room has exactly one correct answer. If two candidates look right, something distinguishes them. Find what.
