NEXUS Audit Signer - Export Notes
Room 7 // signer export v1

The NEXUS audit signer signs every log entry with ECDSA. This export holds eighty of its signatures and one sealed vault. Everything is hex. Nothing here says how to read it; that part is yours.

Signature scheme

itemvalue
curvesecp256k1 (order n = 0xFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFEBAAEDCE6AF48A03BBFD25E8CD0364141)
public keypub = [x, y], in the export
messagethe text in msg, as UTF-8 bytes
digesth = SHA-256(msg), read as a big-endian integer
signature(r, s) with s = k^-1 (h + r d) mod n, where d is the private key and k is the per-signature nonce
entriesnightjar-log-entry-0 to nightjar-log-entry-79

Vault

itemvalue
cipherAES-256-GCM
keySHA-256 of d, where d is written as 32 bytes, big-endian
nonce12 bytes, hex, in enc.nonce
ciphertexthex, in enc.ct
tag16 bytes, hex, in enc.tag
associated datanone
plaintexta flag in NIGHTJAR{...} form

Answer formats

targetformat
7.1NIGHTJAR{nonce_bits_N}, where N is the bit length that every nonce stays under
7.2NIGHTJAR{d_XXXXXXXXXXXXXXXX}, the first 16 hex digits of d, lowercase
7.3the flag stored in the vault
7.4NIGHTJAR{k17_XXXXXXXX}, the last 8 hex digits of the nonce used by nightjar-log-entry-17, lowercase

Check any candidate private key against the public key before you trust it. Several wrong ones look right.
